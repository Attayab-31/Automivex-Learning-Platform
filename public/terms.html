There is no application, tuition, assessment or certificate fee charged by Automivex for the current learning path. The linked freeCodeCamp course is owned and published by its respective creator. Automivex does not claim authorship or ownership of that video. The Automivex learning path provides independent organisation, milestones, an assessment and a separate project brief. External content may change or become unavailable.

Academic integrity and project ownership

You must submit work that you created or have permission to publish. You may use libraries, documentation and properly attributed third-party resources. Do not submit another person's project as your own, include secrets or personal data in public repositories, or violate third-party licences. You retain ownership of your original project; submitting a repository URL does not transfer its intellectual property to Automivex.

Automated repository checks

The automated repository checker inspects public GitHub metadata and the presence of expected files. It does not execute submitted code, perform a security audit, or measure all aspects of code quality. A passing checklist is followed by a separate administrator review before certificate issuance. Neither step proves that a project is production-ready.

Certificates

A certificate is a course-completion record issued after the published learning milestones, assessment threshold, automated repository checklist and administrator approval are satisfied. It is not a professional licence, accredited qualification, job guarantee or independent expert validation. Automivex may correct or revoke a certificate issued due to technical error, fraud or material misrepresentation.

Community conduct

WhatsApp and other community participation is optional. Be respectful, do not share other learners' private information, avoid spam, harassment, plagiarism or harmful links, and follow the third-party platform's rules. Automivex may restrict access to community spaces for abuse.

Availability and changes

The platform is provided without a paid service-level agreement. Features, courses and hosting availability may change. We will make reasonable efforts to keep learner records and verification available but do not guarantee uninterrupted service.
